Wajdi Zaghouani — Personal Faculty Website (Static HTML Build)

To preview: double-click index.html in any browser.

Pages:
  index.html          Home (bio + contact only)
  research.html       Research areas
  publications.html   Links to Google Scholar and ACL Anthology
  teaching.html       Courses and graduate supervision
  cv.html             Research metrics, summary, CV download
  dashboard.html      Interactive research dashboard (Scholar data, datasets, CV)

Assets:
  img/                Portrait photo
  docs/               Wajdi_Zaghouani_CV.pdf

Updating the dashboard:
  Open dashboard.html from your computer (double-click). The "Update data" button
  appears only then. Paste a fresh copy of the Google Scholar profile page, press
  "Download updated dashboard", rename the downloaded file to dashboard.html and
  replace this one. The site header and footer are part of the file, so they stay.
